================================================================================
CONCLUSION
================================================================================

The FAB simulator engine has good support for equipment activation and hero
triggers, but critically lacks support for the broader card effect ecosystem.

Nearly 60% of cards in generated decks have complex game effects that are not
currently implemented. This represents a major architectural gap that must be
addressed to fully support the card pool.

The most urgent needs are:
  1. Token creation system
  2. On-hit/on-attack effect system
  3. Condition tracking for "if you've..." checks
  4. Card modification system
  5. Draw/deck manipulation system
